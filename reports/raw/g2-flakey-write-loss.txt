=== G2: host-level write loss (dm-flakey drop_writes), WSL2/Linux ===

The same probe as experiments/redis_durability_window.py -- two keys, one
WAITAOF-acknowledged and one not -- with the fault swapped from a process
kill to a block device that stops accepting writes.
