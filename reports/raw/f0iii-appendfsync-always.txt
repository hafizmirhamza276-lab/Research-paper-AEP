==============================================================================
Barrier cost as a function of durability policy -- AEP_FULL
==============================================================================
appendfsync     runs   exec   median ms     p95 ms   over 2000ms floor
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
everysec           3     30      4004.9     6892.9              2004.9
always             3     30      2063.4     5067.8                63.4
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Two WAITAOF barriers cost 2004.9 ms under everysec and 63.4 ms under always.
Difference: 1941.5 ms.

The everysec figure is the one a reader would otherwise take as 'AEP's overhead'.
It is the cost of waiting for a scheduled fsync that is up to one second away,
not the cost of the protocol. The protocol minus the barrier costs 28 ms
(B3 versus B0, same cell family).
