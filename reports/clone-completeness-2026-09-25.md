**Nothing in the paper derives from it**, and `check_prereg_order.py` cannot see

**The defect.** The Makefile target passed nine `--` inputs; `check_paper_numbers.py`
passes ten. Diffing the two flag sets:

```
$ comm -23 <gate's flags> <Makefile's flags>
--abd-immediate
```

Run as the Makefile had it, the generator produced a `numbers.tex` with **0**
`\Abd*` macros against the committed file's **11**, and `numbers.tex` **DIFFERS**.
Reproduced directly before fixing.

**This is the second occurrence of the same drift.** The Makefile's own comment
records phase 25: four inputs reached `paper_tables.py` and not this target, and
`reproduce-figures` reported DIFFERS for four passes because *"check_paper_numbers.py
builds its own invocation and never consulted this one — two callers of the same
generator, drifting apart."* Phase 53 added `--abd-immediate` to the generator and
to the gate and not to the target.

**Fixed**, and the comment now records the recurrence. After the fix:

```
Abd macros regenerated: 11
  IDENTICAL numbers.tex
  IDENTICAL table-ablation.tex
  IDENTICAL table-ambiguity-by-crashpoint.tex
  IDENTICAL table-deployment-choice.tex
  IDENTICAL table-latency.tex
```

**There is still no check that the two callers agree.** A gate would be a dozen
lines — parse both flag sets, assert equality — and it does not exist. Until it
does, this will drift a third time.
